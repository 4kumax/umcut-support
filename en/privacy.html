Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy applies to the iPhone app UmCut: Speech Video Editor / UmCut: Монтаж речи (bundle identifier app.umcut.free), displayed on the device as UmCut Free.

Publisher: Max Kuznetsoff. Privacy contact: umcut@proton.me.

Processing on your iPhone

UmCut Free edits videos you select from Photos or Files. Video, audio and speech transcripts are processed locally using models included in the app. The app does not create an account or automatically upload your videos, transcripts or editing history to the publisher.

Data stored by the app

The app keeps copies of imported videos, edits, transcripts and exported results in its private storage so you can reopen projects. It also stores your language preference. Delete a project from history to remove the app's copies. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Files already exported to Photos, Files or another app must be removed separately; deleting a project does not delete your original recording.

Permissions and sharing

The system Photos and Files pickers give access to items you choose. The app asks for permission to add to Photos only when you save a video there. You can deny or revoke that permission in iOS Settings and use the system share sheet instead. Sharing sends the selected file to the destination you choose, whose privacy rules apply.

Tracking and service providers

UmCut Free includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Core editing needs no cloud AI service. Apple may process information under its own policies when you use the App Store, iOS backups, iCloud Photos or other system services. Your iOS backup settings may include app data. A file stored only in iCloud or another Files provider may require a system download before editing.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, Max Kuznetsoff receives your email address, message and any attachments you send and uses them to answer your request. No video or diagnostic report is sent automatically. Include only information needed to explain the issue.

The support mailbox uses Proton Mail. Email delivery involves metadata such as sender and recipient addresses, subject and message times; provider processing is described in the Proton Mail privacy policy and Proton Privacy Policy. Your own email provider also applies its policies.

Support correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing umcut@proton.me. The request concerns copies controlled by the publisher; providers handle their own records under their policies.

This website

This static support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages do not set cookies or include advertising, analytics scripts or external fonts. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub account. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for provider processing, retention and privacy requests. The publisher does not control GitHub's security-log retention.

Your choices and changes

You can remove local projects, revoke Photos access, and delete separately exported files. For support-message or website-data requests, contact the address above. If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.
