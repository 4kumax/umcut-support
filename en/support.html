Support

UmCut is a free, offline editor for spoken videos on iPhone. Russian and English are available in the app's settings.

Getting started

  1. Choose the language spoken in your video.
  2. Import a video from Photos or Files and analyze it.
  3. Review suggested cuts and captions.
  4. Export and save to Photos or share with another app.

Common questions

Does it cost anything? All editing features are free, with no subscription, watermark or export quota.

Why are some words wrong? Speech recognition is approximate. Background noise, music and overlapping speakers can reduce accuracy. Review suggestions and correct captions before exporting.

Can it work offline? Yes, with media already available on your device. A file that exists only in iCloud or a remote Files provider must first be downloaded by iOS.

Saving to Photos was denied. Allow adding photos in iOS Settings or use the share sheet to save to Files.

A large file fails. Check free storage, try a shorter copy and keep the app open during processing. Supported formats and available memory determine what can be processed.

How do I delete a project? Open history and delete it. The original recording and files already exported outside the app remain.
